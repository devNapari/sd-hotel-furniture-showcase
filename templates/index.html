About Our Company

Quality Furniture & Affordable Construction

AM AWUNI FURNITURE & CONSTRUCTION specializes in manufacturing and selling furniture of all types including sofa sets, dining tables, complete room decorations, and kitchen setups. We also provide affordable construction services for homes, offices, schools, and roads.

Located in Tamale, Tuutingli on the Second Ring Road near Dabokpa Technical Institute, we serve customers both locally and nationally with plans to extend internationally within five years. What makes us unique is our use of eco-friendly materials, combining both local and foreign materials, while providing flexible payment terms.
