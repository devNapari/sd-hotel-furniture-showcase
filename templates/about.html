About AM AWUNI FURNITURE & CONSTRUCTION

Crafting excellence in hospitality furniture for over two decades

Our Story

Your Vision, Our Craftsmanship

For over two decades, AM AWUNI FURNITURE & CONSTRUCTION has been a trusted partner for hoteliers and designers worldwide. We specialize in creating high-quality, custom-made furniture that not only meets the aesthetic and functional needs of the hospitality industry but also tells a story.

Our commitment to excellence is reflected in every piece we create, from elegant guest room furnishings to stunning public area installations. We believe in building lasting relationships with our clients through collaboration, innovation, and unwavering dedication to quality.

Our Mission

To deliver exceptional, custom-designed furniture solutions that enhance the hospitality experience, combining timeless elegance with modern functionality. We strive to exceed our clients' expectations through superior craftsmanship, innovative design, and dedicated service.

Our Vision

To be the world's leading provider of hospitality furniture, recognized for our commitment to quality, sustainability, and innovation. We envision a future where every hotel space we furnish becomes a testament to exceptional design and enduring craftsmanship.
